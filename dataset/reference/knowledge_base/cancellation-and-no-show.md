# Cancellation and No-Show Policy

## Cancelling an Appointment

Appointments can be cancelled at no charge through Meridian Assist, by phone, or in person, up
until the scheduled appointment time. We ask for as much notice as possible so the time slot
can be offered to another customer.

## Rescheduling

Appointments can be rescheduled to any available time at no charge. Same-day rescheduling is
subject to availability and requires at least 2 hours' notice before the original appointment
time.

## No-Show Policy

A no-show is recorded when a customer does not arrive within 30 minutes of their scheduled
appointment time without cancelling. After 2 no-shows within a 12-month period, same-day
online and assistant booking may be temporarily limited to phone booking with a service
advisor; this does not affect your ability to receive service.

## Recall and Warranty Appointments

There is never a cancellation or no-show fee for recall or warranty appointments.

## Late Arrival

If you expect to arrive more than 15 minutes late, please call your location; we will do our
best to accommodate you, but your original time slot may need to be adjusted based on bay and
technician availability.
