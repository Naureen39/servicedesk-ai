# Loaner Cars and Shuttle Service

## Loaner Vehicles

A limited number of complimentary loaner vehicles are available at the Riverside and Northgate
locations for repairs expected to take longer than 4 hours, subject to availability. Loaner
cars are not guaranteed and are reserved in the order requests are received.

To reserve a loaner, you must:
- Be at least 21 years old with a valid driver's license
- Provide proof of insurance
- Leave a card on file for the security hold (not charged unless the loaner is damaged or
  returned with less fuel than provided)

Westerville does not currently stock loaner vehicles; customers at that location are offered
shuttle service or a rideshare credit for qualifying repairs.

## Shuttle Service

All three locations offer a complimentary local shuttle within a 10-mile radius during business
hours for customers who prefer to wait at home or work rather than in the lobby. Shuttle
requests should be made at check-in; wait times vary by time of day.

## Waiting Area

Each location has a customer lounge with WiFi, coffee, and charging stations for repairs
expected to take under 90 minutes.

## Rideshare Credit

For same-day repairs when a loaner and shuttle are both unavailable, Meridian Auto Group can
issue a rideshare credit up to $25 each way at the service advisor's discretion.
