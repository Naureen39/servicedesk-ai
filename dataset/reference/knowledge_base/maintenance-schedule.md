# Maintenance Schedule

General guidance for most gasoline passenger vehicles. Always follow the interval printed in
your owner's manual, since it can vary by manufacturer, engine, and driving conditions.

## Every 5,000 to 7,500 Miles
- Conventional oil and filter change
- Tire rotation
- Multi-point inspection (brakes, fluids, belts, lights)

## Every 7,500 to 10,000 Miles (Synthetic Oil)
- Full synthetic oil and filter change
- Tire rotation
- Multi-point inspection

## Every 15,000 Miles
- Cabin and engine air filter inspection or replacement
- Brake inspection

## Every 30,000 Miles
- 30K scheduled service: fluid top-offs, filter replacements, drive belt inspection,
  suspension and steering inspection

## Every 60,000 Miles
- 60K scheduled service: spark plugs (where applicable), transmission fluid service,
  coolant inspection, brake fluid inspection, comprehensive inspection

## Every 90,000 Miles
- 90K scheduled service: timing components inspection (where applicable), coolant flush,
  drive belt replacement, comprehensive inspection

## Severe Driving Conditions

Frequent short trips, towing, dusty roads, extreme temperatures, or stop-and-go traffic call
for shorter intervals than listed above. Ask your service advisor whether your driving pattern
qualifies as severe.
