# Payment and Financing

## Accepted Payment Methods

Meridian Auto Group accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, debit cards, cash,
and certified checks at all three locations. Contactless payment (Apple Pay, Google Pay) is
also accepted.

## Financing Options

For repairs over $500, customers may apply for a promotional financing line through our
third-party financing partner, subject to credit approval. Standard promotional terms include
6 months same-as-cash on approved purchases; ask your service advisor for current terms, since
promotions change periodically.

## Payment Timing

Payment is due at pickup unless the repair is covered by warranty, an active recall, or an
approved extended service contract. A deposit may be requested for special-order parts.

## Estimates and Approval

No repair beyond the initial diagnostic is performed without customer approval of a written
estimate. If additional issues are found during a repair, the service advisor will contact you
before performing any additional work or charging for it.

## Fleet and Commercial Accounts

Businesses with 3 or more vehicles may apply for a Meridian fleet account with consolidated
monthly billing and priority scheduling. Contact any location's service manager to apply.

## Receipts

Itemized digital receipts are emailed automatically after payment; printed receipts are
available on request at checkout.
