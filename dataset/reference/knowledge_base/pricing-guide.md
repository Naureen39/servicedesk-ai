## Recall and Warranty Work

Recall repairs are always performed at no cost to the customer. Warranty-covered repairs carry
no customer charge beyond any applicable deductible on an extended service contract; see
`warranty-coverage.md`.

## Diagnostic Fee Waiver

If a diagnostic identifies a repair that you approve and have completed at Meridian Auto
Group the same visit, the diagnostic fee is waived and only the repair is charged.

## Estimates Are Not Final Invoices

Prices above are planning estimates. Actual pricing can vary by location, vehicle
configuration, and current parts and labor rates. Ask your service advisor for a written
estimate specific to your vehicle before authorizing work.
