# Privacy Notice

## Information We Collect

When you use Meridian Assist by chat or voice, we collect the information needed to answer
your question or complete your request: your name, phone number, vehicle details, appointment
information, and the conversation transcript. Voice conversations are converted to text; the
audio recording is deleted immediately after transcription and is never stored.

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to look up recall and complaint data, provide pricing and warranty
guidance, schedule and manage appointments, and improve response quality. We do not sell your
personal information.

## Third-Party Data Sources

Recall and complaint information is retrieved from the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety
Administration (NHTSA), a public government source. No personal information is sent to NHTSA;
only vehicle year, make, model, or VIN is used for lookups.

## Language Model Providers

To answer questions that require natural-language understanding beyond our built-in systems,
Meridian Assist may send a limited, redacted portion of your message to a third-party language
model provider. Before this happens, personal information such as your phone number, email
address, full VIN, and name is automatically removed or masked. Vehicle safety and recall
answers are always generated from verified data, not invented by the language model.

## Data Retention

Conversation transcripts are retained to support your appointment and any follow-up. Transcripts
older than 180 days are anonymized by removing names, phone numbers, and other direct
identifiers while preserving the conversation content for quality review.

## Your Choices

You may ask a service advisor at any time to have your contact information corrected or
removed from marketing communications. Appointment and repair history required for warranty
and legal record-keeping cannot be deleted while your vehicle is under active warranty.

## Contact

Questions about this notice can be directed to any Meridian Auto Group service location listed
in `hours-and-locations.md`.
