# Warranty Coverage

## Manufacturer Warranty

Most new vehicles serviced at Meridian Auto Group carry a manufacturer bumper-to-bumper
warranty of 3 years or 36,000 miles, and a powertrain warranty of 5 years or 60,000 miles,
whichever comes first. Coverage details vary by manufacturer and model year, so our service
advisors confirm exact terms using your vehicle's VIN before any warranty repair.

Bumper-to-bumper coverage generally includes electrical systems, air conditioning, fuel
systems, and most mechanical components except regular wear items.

Powertrain coverage includes the engine, transmission, transfer case, and drive axle
components.

## What Is Not Covered

Routine maintenance items are not covered by warranty, including oil changes, tire rotation,
brake pad wear, wiper blades, cabin and engine air filters, and alignment adjustments not
caused by a manufacturing defect. Damage from accidents, misuse, or lack of routine
maintenance is also excluded.

## Recall Repairs

Safety recall repairs are always performed at no cost to the customer, regardless of the
vehicle's warranty status or mileage, for as long as the recall campaign remains open. This is
required by federal law and is separate from the manufacturer's standard warranty.

## Extended Service Contracts

Customers who purchased an extended service contract through Meridian Auto Group or a
third-party provider should bring their contract number to their appointment. Our advisors
will verify coverage and any deductible before starting work.

## Warranty Claims Process

1. The service advisor identifies the concern and confirms it is warranty-eligible.
2. A diagnostic is performed, at no charge if the concern is covered.
3. If approved, the repair is completed and billed to the manufacturer or contract provider.
4. The customer receives a copy of the repair order showing the warranty coverage applied.

If a repair is not warranty-eligible, the advisor will provide a written estimate before any
customer-pay work begins.
